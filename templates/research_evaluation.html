Research interpretation: The adaptive method is evaluated without assuming it is superior. Methodological Strengths

What Phase 8 adds to the research methodology.

Leakage control

Outer test folds are never used to choose adaptive weights. Weight selection uses an inner validation procedure on the outer training data.

Stability

Three repeated 5-fold evaluations reduce dependence on a single train/test split and report variability using standard deviation.

Probability quality

Brier score and calibration curves evaluate probability behaviour separately from classification accuracy.

Fair comparison

Static probability averaging and sample-adaptive weighting are evaluated under the same repeated outer folds.
