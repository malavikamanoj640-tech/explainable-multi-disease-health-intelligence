ABOUT THE PLATFORM

From Health Data to Meaningful Insights

AI Health Risk Assessment is a multi-disease health intelligence platform designed to use artificial intelligence and machine learning for potential health risk assessment.

The platform allows users to provide health information manually or upload supported medical reports. Relevant information can then be extracted, validated and processed for AI-assisted assessment.

The system goes beyond simply displaying a prediction. It presents the assessment with confidence information, contributing risk factors, model analysis and personalized recommendations to make the result easier to understand.

Users can also maintain their assessment history, review previous results and interact with an AI health assistant for additional understanding of their assessment.
